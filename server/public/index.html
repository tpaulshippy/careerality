Why Careerality

Career guidance that's honest, data-driven, and personal

Traditional career advice is either vague ("follow your passion") or disconnected from financial reality. Careerality bridges the gap.

Real ROI Data

Every career shows its actual return on investment: median salary, education cost, break-even timeline, and cost-of-living adjusted earnings. Built on BLS, O*NET, and IPEDS data.

Day-in-the-Life Narratives

Not generic job descriptions — authentic snapshots of what a real workday feels like. See the tradeoffs, the routine, and the human reality before you invest years in a path.

Swipe to Explore

An intuitive dating-style interface that makes career exploration feel natural. Swipe right to explore further, left to skip. The more you swipe, the better your recommendations.

Smart Filtering

Filter by state, salary range, and education pathway. Sort by ROI, salary, break-even speed, or job market demand. Find exactly what matters to you.

Location-Aware

Salary and cost-of-living are adjusted for where you actually live. A $60K salary in Mississippi is very different from $60K in San Francisco — we show you the difference.

Market Demand

See projected job growth, annual openings, and demand rankings so you know whether a career path has a future — not just a present.

How It Works

From curiosity to clarity in minutes

1

Swipe through careers

Browse real career paths one card at a time. Each shows a day-in-the-life narrative, salary data, education cost, and break-even timeline.

2

Tell us what you like

After swiping right, tap what interested you — salary, environment, skills, security, or balance. This trains the algorithm to surface better matches.

3

Review your liked careers

Your right-swiped careers live in the Liked tab, ready to compare. See salary, ROI%, and break-even at a glance for every career you're considering.

4

Dive into the details

Tap any career for the full picture: day-in-the-life narrative, education requirements, skills breakdown, market demand, and career images.
