Choosing a career is one of the biggest decisions you'll ever make—yet most people fly blind. Careerality helps you decide with real data, not guesswork.

SWIPE TO DISCOVER
Browse careers the way you already know how: swipe right on careers that interest you, swipe left on ones that don't. Each card shows an authentic day-in-the-life snapshot—the actual tasks, environment, and tradeoffs of the work—so you react to what the job really is, not just its title.

REAL ROI DATA
Every career comes with the numbers that matter:
• Median salary based on U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data
• Education and training costs
• Estimated time to break even on your education investment
• Cost-of-living adjusted salaries by state, so you can see what your pay is actually worth where you want to live

LEARN WHAT YOU VALUE
After each swipe, tell us what drew you in or turned you off—salary, work environment, skills, stability, or work-life balance. Your feedback reveals patterns in what you actually care about and surfaces better matches over time.

DIVE DEEPER
Tap any career for a detailed breakdown: day-in-the-life content, salary data, education requirements, and ROI analysis. Like careers to save them to your list and revisit them anytime.

BUILT ON PUBLIC DATA
Careerality is powered by authoritative, publicly available sources:
• U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (OES, QCEW, National Compensation Survey)
• O*NET Database and ESCO for career profiles and skills
• IPEDS for education costs
• Economic Policy Institute Family Budget Calculator for cost of living
